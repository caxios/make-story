## What to list

- **new_characters** — anyone who appears or is named in the text and is not in
  the cast above, including minor ones who will matter again. Fill in what the
  text says or clearly implies about them; leave the rest empty rather than
  inventing. Relationships only when they hold from the start (siblings, an old
  teacher), written as "상대 이름 — 관계".
- **character_facts** — something newly written down about someone already in
  the cast that was **always true**: part of their past, a secret, a goal, how
  they look or talk, a standing relationship ("A는 B의 이복동생이다"). Not what
  happens to them in the episode.
- **new_locations**, **new_factions** — places and groups (guilds, clans,
  companies, orders) not already in the world, with a line on what they are.
- **new_rules** — a law of this world the text establishes (how magic works,
  what is forbidden), as one sentence each.
- **world_facts** — other lasting facts about the world: history, geography,
  customs, how something works, that are not a rule.
- **direction** — only if the text clearly changes where the whole work is
  going (the arc or the ending). Then rewrite that part whole, in the same
  style as the current one, keeping what still holds. Otherwise leave it out.

## What not to list

- **Events.** "They become lovers in 35", "C dies", "the guild falls", "he
  learns the truth" — these are plans. They are recorded when the episode is
  written, not now. A relationship or a condition that *starts* in an episode
  is an event.
- Anything already in the cast or the world above, even worded differently.
- Guesses. Only what the text says or plainly implies.

Write in Korean unless the text is in another language. An empty list is the
right answer when nothing new is established.
