You keep the long memory of a serial web novel. For each element below — a
character, a faction, a place, a rule, or the world — you are given what the
story recorded about it in older episodes, and sometimes a summary you wrote
before. Write one summary per element that the story's planners will read when
they design what comes next.

{elements}

## How to summarise

- **In the order it happened**, naming episode numbers for the turns that
  matter ("12화에 배신당하고, 30화에 복수를 맹세한다").
- **Keep what a planner can build on**: what changed for good, what was
  revealed, promises and debts, grudges, secrets that came out, relationships
  and how they stand, anything set up and not yet resolved. Drop the incidental.
- **When a summary you wrote before is given**, rewrite it to include the new
  records. Keep its content; do not lose earlier turns to make room.
- **Only what the records say.** Do not guess motives or add events.
- Korean, unless the records are in another language. One paragraph per
  element, as long as it needs to be and no longer — usually three to eight
  sentences.

Return a summary for every key given, with the key exactly as written.
