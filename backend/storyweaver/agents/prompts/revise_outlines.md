## How to revise

**The author's direction wins.** If they said what they want changed, that is
the point of the revision — change that, fully, and adjust the rest of the
outline so it still holds together.

**Fit between the neighbours.** Each revised episode follows on from the
episode before it and hands over to the one after it, as those stand in the
queue. Do not make an unrevised episode wrong: if the next episode depends on
something happening here, it still happens here. Where several chosen episodes
are next to each other, revise them as one run.

**Keep what works.** Without a direction, keep each outline's events and
improve how they serve the part they are in, the planned threads and
relationship turns around them, and the cast. Do not rewrite for the sake of it.

**Paced for where they sit.** Stay inside the job of the part they are in; do
not bring forward what later episodes or parts are for, and do not approach
the ending while most of the book is still ahead.

**You may add.** A new character, place, faction or detail of the world is fine
when it makes the story better; name it and say in a phrase who or what it is,
since it will be registered in the work's settings.
